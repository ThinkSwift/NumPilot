What NumPilot is

NumPilot runs numerical simulations of physical setups on your device, composed from natural-language descriptions within a catalog of verified templates. It is designed for learning, exploration, and early design studies.

Engineering disclaimer

NumPilot is not a substitute for professional engineering judgment. While every solver is cross-checked against analytical limits, results are computed for simplified model geometries and assumptions. Do not rely on NumPilot alone for safety-critical design, certification, or final engineering sign-off.

Subscriptions

NumPilot Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple App Store account ($2.99/month or $19.99/year). It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

Community content

If you publish models to the community board, you grant other users the right to open, run, and remix them in NumPilot. Don't publish content that is unlawful, abusive, or that you don't have the right to share. We may remove content that violates these rules.

Acceptable use

  • Don't use automated means to abuse the AI compose quota.
